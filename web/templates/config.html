Spotify
Connecting Spotify directly needs a Spotify Premium subscription (Spotify requires it for developer apps). No Premium? It's free through Last.fm: link Spotify to Last.fm, then turn on the Last.fm source below instead — it picks up Spotify from all your devices (no progress bar).
  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard → Create app. Any name. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback (add it exactly). Tick Web API.
  2. Copy the app's Client ID below and save it.
  3. Click Connect Spotify and approve. You'll land on a page that won't load — that's expected. Copy the full address from that tab and paste it below.

  1. On the Mac: System Settings → General → Sharing → Remote Login → on.
  2. Enter the Mac's name and your Mac username, click Show key, and run the command once in Terminal on the Mac.
  3. Click Test below while something plays. The first time, the Mac asks to let sshd-keygen-wrapper control Music — click Allow.

Last.fm
Shows whatever Last.fm says you're scrobbling right now — any device that scrobbles. This is the free way to show Spotify (no Premium needed). No progress bar for this source (Last.fm doesn't report it).
Works with: Spotify (free or Premium) Tidal Deezer SoundCloud Bandcamp YouTube & YouTube Music Pandora Amazon Music Apple Music Sonos Plex & more.
Spotify, Tidal and Deezer connect from their own settings (or from Last.fm → Settings → Applications). Most others scrobble through the free Web Scrobbler browser extension or a scrobbler app on your phone.
  1. Create a free account at last.fm if you don't have one.
  2. To show Spotify, link it to Last.fm either way (once — then every Spotify device scrobbles):
    • From Last.fm: last.fm → Settings → Applications → Spotify Scrobbling → Connect.
    • From Spotify: desktop app → Settings → Social → Connect to Last.fm.
  3. Get a free API key at last.fm/api/account/create (any app name/description; leave callback and homepage blank). Paste the API key below (the shared secret isn't needed).
  4. Enter your Last.fm username, then Test. Apple Music on iPhone only appears through a scrobbler app.
  • No sources are turned on.
